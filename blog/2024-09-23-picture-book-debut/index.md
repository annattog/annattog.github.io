I’m thrilled to announce the release of my debut picture book, **"[くつ下をクローゼットの下にいれないでね](https://amzn.to/4dXbGNF)"** ("Don't Put Socks Under the Closet"). After much hard work and dedication, this heartwarming story is finally available for everyone to enjoy.

<!--truncate-->

This book is special to me because it’s not just a story—it's an opportunity for parents, carers, and children to share a fun and meaningful reading experience. The tale of misplaced socks and childhood curiosity is something we can all relate to, and I hope it brings a smile to the faces of those who read it.

**Share Your Thoughts!**

On the last page of the book, you’ll find something extra—a **QR code** that links to a short **[questionnaire](https://forms.gle/GwjgxoS7wZFTwWuZA)**. I would love to hear your thoughts about the book! Your feedback means the world to me and will help me in creating even more enjoyable stories in the future. Whether you're a parent reading it to your little one or a child enjoying the adventure yourself, every response counts.

Please take a moment to scan the QR code and let me know what you think! Your feedback will not only help me improve but also shape the future of children's literature by creating stories that kids love.

Thank you for your support, and I can’t wait to hear from you!
